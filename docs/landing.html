Why Saolrian

Tracking tools stopped being yours.

Every calorie tracker wants your data on their servers, behind their paywall, feeding their ad business. Saolrian takes the other road.

Own your data

Self-host the whole app with a single binary, or use our hosted tier. Export everything, anytime, in CSV. No lock-in, no if-and-when.

Everything in one place

Meals, macros, weights and goals today — with Strava, Liftosaur and Health Connect feeding one dashboard next.

Calm by design

No ads, no dark patterns, no engagement traps. A tracker that respects your attention and your evenings.

Features

Everything the MVP needs, nothing it doesn't.

Barcode scanning

Scan a product, get full macros from Open Food Facts — an open database of 3M+ foods.

Custom meal slots

Breakfast, second breakfast, pre-workout — name your meals whatever you want, add as many as you like.

TDEE & macro goals

Mifflin-St Jeor or Katch-McArdle, five activity levels, lose/maintain/gain with live macro targets.

Lose It! import

Your history comes with you. Import your Lose It! export in one pass and keep your streaks alive.

History & trends

Week strips with adherence dots, daily budgets, and weight trends against your goal line.

Make it yours

Eight accent palettes plus a custom picker — themed live, saved per user and per instance.

Offline-first PWA

Install it from the browser. Log meals on the plane — entries sync when you land.

Open source, MIT

Audit it, fork it, patch it. The whole stack is code you can read.

Self-hosting is a first-class feature

One binary. Your server. Done.

Self-hosting shouldn't mean a weekend of Docker archaeology. Saolrian ships as a single Go binary — database, auth, storage, admin UI and all. If you can run a file, you can run Saolrian.

01

Download one binary

~15 MB. Linux, macOS, Windows, ARM — whatever's under your desk or in your NAS.

02

Run it

One command starts the server. Your data lives in a single SQLite file — back it up with Litestream and sleep well.

03

Point the app at your URL

First launch asks: hosted or self-hosted? Choose yours, enter the URL, sign in. Same app, your hardware.

$ ./saolrian serve --http 0.0.0.0:8090
✔ server started at http://127.0.0.1:8090
✔ admin UI at http://127.0.0.1:8090/_/ # create your admin on first visit
✔ data: ./pb_data/saolrian.db (one SQLite file — yours)
$ litestream replicate ./pb_data/saolrian.db s3://your-bucket # optional backups
